# AI Production Harness

**Independent R&D · Reusable AI engineering framework**

## The problem

A working AI demonstration still needs a way to assess output quality, understand failures and manage its behaviour before it can support dependable operations.

## What I built

Developed a reusable engineering framework focused on the reliability, evaluation, monitoring and governance of AI-powered applications and autonomous agents.

## Why it matters

The project brings operational questions into AI development: how to assess results, observe behaviour, respond to failure and maintain human accountability. The framework is an independent R&D project focused on production engineering.

## Capabilities demonstrated

- Applying evaluation and quality assurance to AI applications.
- Considering reliability and failure handling.
- Using monitoring to support operational understanding.
- Making governance and human accountability part of delivery.

Source code, control design, evaluation implementation, configuration and proprietary methods remain private.
