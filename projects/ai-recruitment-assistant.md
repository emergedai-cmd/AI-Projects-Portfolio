# AI Recruitment Assistant

**Enterprise production implementation · HR decision support**

## The problem

Manual CV review created repetitive work for HR and made it harder to compare candidates consistently against role requirements.

## What I delivered

Designed, built and deployed a bespoke desktop application linking recruitment information with AI-assisted candidate review. It compares CVs with role requirements and generates shortlisting recommendations for human review. The wider solution also supports candidate communications and interview invitations.

## Outcome

Adopted by HR across an organisation of approximately 200 staff, reducing manual CV review effort by approximately 60%. Recommendations remain subject to human review; the application supports decision-making rather than making final hiring decisions.

## Capabilities demonstrated

- Translating an HR workflow into a working AI solution.
- Integrating business systems and relevant information.
- Delivering measurable reductions in manual work.
- Retaining human accountability in a consequential workflow.
