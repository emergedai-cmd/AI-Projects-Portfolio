# Automated Onboarding & Offboarding

**Enterprise production implementation · Employee lifecycle automation**

## The problem

Employee onboarding and offboarding required coordination across requests, accounts and access. Manual handoffs increased the risk of delays and inconsistent completion.

## What I delivered

Designed and implemented an automated solution connecting employee lifecycle requests with enterprise identity and account processes. It supports more consistent provisioning and access removal across the workflow.

## Outcome

Improved provisioning speed, process consistency, compliance and audit readiness.

## Capabilities demonstrated

- Discovering and improving an operational workflow.
- Connecting enterprise systems into a repeatable process.
- Considering access management and operational accountability.
- Delivering automation within an established IT service environment.
