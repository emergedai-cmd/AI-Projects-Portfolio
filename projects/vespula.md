# Vespula — Autonomous Agent Platform

**Independent R&D**

## The problem

Tasks involving several activities need coordination, reliable execution and a clear place for human oversight.

## What I built

Designed and developed an experimental multi-agent platform supporting task coordination, multiple model providers, fallback and human approval of actions.

## Why it matters

Vespula explores how autonomous assistance can be organised around a broader task while retaining human oversight. The project remains an independent R&D build.

## Capabilities demonstrated

- Designing a coherent application around coordinated AI agents.
- Considering model availability and continuity of execution.
- Including human approval in agent-driven work.
- Developing an independent AI product through experimentation.

Source code, detailed architecture, task decomposition methods, prompts and configuration remain private.
